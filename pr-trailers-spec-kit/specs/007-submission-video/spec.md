# Spec · 007 — Submission video (HyperFrames), rehearsal and submit

**Estado:** Borrador · **Origin:** hackathon plan · **Priority:** High · **Depends:** 006
**Budget:** 60 min (5:00–6:00). No new product code after 5:00 (Art. II).

## Why
The *Best Demo Video Using HyperFrames* prize is judged on the project's demo video. Our strongest story is dogfooding: **the demo video is built with HyperFrames and its centerpiece is a real trailer the product generated.** This hour also covers the live demo rehearsal and the submission itself.

## User stories
- **US-1.** As a judge, I want a short, polished video that explains the problem, shows the product working, and makes me remember it.
- **US-2.** As the presenter, I want a rehearsed 3-minute live demo with a recorded backup, so nothing breaks on stage.
- **US-3.** As the presenter, I want the submission done before the deadline, with every prize category selected.

## Functional requirements (EARS)
- **FR-1.** The submission video SHALL be rendered with HyperFrames from an HTML composition in `video/submission/`.
- **FR-2.** The video SHALL include, in order: hook, problem, solution, how it works, the real PR #1 trailer embedded, the resulting PR comment, the tech used (Gemini + HyperFrames), and an outro with the tagline "Diffs show you the code. PR Trailers show you the change."
- **FR-3.** The video SHALL include on-screen captions for all narration text (it must work without sound).
- **FR-4.** The video SHALL say explicitly, on screen, that the trailer inside it was generated by the product.
- **FR-5.** The video length SHALL respect the hackathon's limit.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `video/submission/out.mp4` rendered with HyperFrames, 60–120 s (or within the official limit).
- [ ] The live demo was rehearsed at least 3 times end to end, under 3 minutes.
- [ ] A backup screen recording of a full successful run exists.
- [ ] The submission is sent with the repo link (public, with README), the video, and the prize categories: Best Overall, HyperFrames, Gemini.

## Out of scope
New pipeline features, refactors.
